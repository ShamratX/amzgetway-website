Amazon Listing Optimization

Fully optimized product listing is a major factor that differentiates a successful seller from an unsuccessful one. Being the foundation of Amazon selling, listing optimization helps you rank higher on searches, boost your sales, and grow your customer base.

When you rank higher on related searches, more and more potential buyers notice your products and click to read your product descriptions. Now, you have only a few seconds to turn the viewers into buyers.

But if you fail to show what the viewers want to see, you’ll lose product ranking along with conversions. Moreover, the algorithm of Amazon changes consistently, making it difficult for sellers to keep up and conduct constant edits. That’s the reason why many sellers lose rankings after some time.

What Is Amazon Listing Optimization?

Listing optimization is the process in which your Amazon listings are improved by employing SEO tactics. Optimized listings get noticed by the Amazon algorithm, and as a result, your products rank higher in the search results.

An Amazon Listing Optimization Service like the one offered by AMZgetway involves upgrading your product pages on Amazon. It includes everything from product titles and keyword research to optimizing listing written content and images.

When done correctly, listing optimization helps you increase traffic, maintain your sales, grow them and drive conversion rates.

What Does Our Amazon Listing Optimization Services Include?

Amazon SEO for Product Titles:

Our service includes the identification and creation of product names and critical keywords. We develop product tiles according to the Amazon guidelines about the product title styling. Our experts make sure all of your product titles are within the character count limit.

Moreover, we remove all of the redundant words and phrases that match Amazon product sub-categories. We use the identified highest-impact keywords in the most logical order.

Amazon Listing Optimization for Bulleted Data:

We will create bullet headers, each including only one keyword. Our specialists ensure that your bulleted data contains and highlights all of the features and distinguishing attributes of your products.

While offering a thorough explanation of the product, we make sure to comply with the character count limit. Furthermore, we incorporate the most critical keywords strategically within your content.

Amazon Product Image Optimization:

Our services include editing and improving the images to make them more visually appealing. We comply with the image size, background, and resolution requirements. To ensure that you have the best-quality images, we also conduct comprehensive market and competitor research.

Amazon SEO Services for Product Description:

Our professionals write highly readable product descriptions and use a storytelling approach to describe your product in the best way. We carefully incorporate both the primary and secondary. If there are any special discounts, product offers, and purchase benefits, we make sure to highlight them in the descriptions.

Amazon SEO Services for Product Category:

Lastly, we expertly check and update your products in the most relevant sub-category. We will also provide you with the report preparation of bestsellers in those product categories.

Our specialists have the skills and the knowledge to determine whether relisting your products under a similar sub-category will boost the visibility of your brand or not. In the end, we provide an analysis of creating highly irresistible product bundles.
